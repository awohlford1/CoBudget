## 1. Purpose and decision rule

This matrix gives every current CBD-12 acceptance criterion a security/privacy
outcome and records every CBD-93 Product Owner input independently. It is a
requirements reconciliation, not implementation evidence.

The four permitted outcomes are:

| Outcome | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pass unchanged | The current product rule is consistent with the approved CBD-14 package and needs no additional CBD-14 condition to remain authoritative. Ordinary implementation evidence may still be required. |
| Pass with mitigation | The current product rule remains authoritative, but its implementation or release is constrained by named CBD-94 requirements, verification, specialist evidence, or scoped release gates. |
| Blocked pending decision/evidence | A material source conflict, Product Owner decision, or required evidence prevents the affected rule or readiness claim from being treated as settled. The current interim behavior is named. |
| Out of CBD-14 scope | The rule is affirmatively governed outside CBD-14 and no security/privacy result is claimed. Absence of analysis is not enough. |

Product decisions approved in CBD-72 and CBD-92 remain controlling. A candidate
`SG-93-*` or `RI-93-*` is not adopted by this matrix. A “Pass with mitigation”
does not mean a release gate has passed; it means the product rule can remain
while the named security work is completed.

## 6. Residual risks and formal acceptance boundary

CBD-95 accepts no new residual risk. The following remain explicit:

- broad authorized role visibility can enable monitoring;
- merchant/payee and sensitive free text can expose non-users and circumstances;
- coercion cannot be reliably inferred from a completed consent ceremony;
- a legitimately authorized person can remember or copy information across
  spaces outside server custody;
- downloaded, forwarded, mirrored, inbox, carrier, provider, and screenshot
  copies cannot be remotely erased;
- archival/deletion/ownership asymmetry can create agency harms even when the
  current product rule is deterministic;
- content-free external notifications still reveal that CoBudget activity
  occurred and may be monitored or suppressed; and
- a solo operator cannot invoke exceptional customer-content access, key
  recovery, privileged recovery, or return-to-service operations. The Product
  Owner requires a genuinely independent second approver; the affected
  production operations remain blocked until that control is implemented and
  exercised.

Formal residual acceptance requires the authority and evidence defined by
CBD-94 §3.6. Document approval does not constitute residual-risk acceptance.
