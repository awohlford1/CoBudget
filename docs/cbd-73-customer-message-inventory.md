## 1. Purpose and contract

This inventory is the versioned catalog of every customer-facing message in the invitation, consent, membership-change, revocation, removal, and ownership-transfer lifecycle. Each row defines the message's audience, trigger, required semantic content, and prohibited content.

This inventory defines **semantics, not strings**. Exact copy, notification templates, template hashes, localization, accessibility evidence, and specialist review remain gated under `FU-95-017` and `EG-91-006`/CBD-106, and no message ships until the Product Owner approves its exact strings. The inventory version participates in consent versioning: the consent-copy version recorded in consent evidence (specification §6, DR-73-04) resolves to a version of the disclosure messages defined here.

## 2. Cross-cutting semantic rules

Every message in §3 obeys all of the following, stated once here and not repeated per row:

1. **Approved `RI-93-016` standard.** Recorded consent or reauthentication is never presented as proof of voluntariness. Channel control is never presented as sole control. Delivered or recipient-controlled copies are never described as remotely revocable or erasable. No message characterizes another person's circumstances or reasons. No message implies authority or accountability the product does not support. Irreversible consequences appear before the action, never only after.
2. **Voluntary, accessible, nonjudgmental.** Consent, decline, and revocation copy is voluntary in tone, meets the inherited SD-071-048 accessibility requirements, avoids blame or judgment, and always includes a safe path to decline or leave (CBD-73-AC13).
3. **Channel ceilings.** Push, SMS, and routine email remain content-free of protected detail; protected detail appears only on authenticated in-app surfaces; purpose-specific email content follows its approved allowlist (August 16, 2026 Product Owner decision under `FU-95-001`).
4. **Non-enumeration.** No message, status, timing difference, or error reveals account existence, a block, a rate limit, verification progress, or another person's private state (specification IC-73-010).
5. **No pre-consent data.** No surface before acceptance shows actual financial data, hidden resources, private member details, or an unauthorized member list (`RI-93-009`).
6. **Personal-state privacy.** No message reveals another member's notification destinations, delivery preferences, alert state, block list, or acceptance-ceremony details.
7. **Terminology.** Role names and lifecycle terms use the approved CBD-72 vocabulary exactly.

## 4. Coverage rule

Every transition and checklist item in the specification that names a customer message resolves to exactly one row above, and every row traces back to at least one transition, checklist item, or governing decision. The traceability record (`docs/cbd-73-acceptance-criteria-traceability.md`) carries the bidirectional check.
